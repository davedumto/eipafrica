TRUST

Credible by design, not by claim.

Large deals depend on who is on the other side. These are the checks the platform is built around.

  • EVERY LISTING

    Reviewed before it goes live

    A listing becomes visible only once our team approves it. The owner can pause or close it at any time.

  • EVERY ORGANISATION

    Verified, with a visible badge

    Organisations submit registration details and, optionally, past project references. Approved ones are marked Verified.

  • CONFIDENTIAL FILES

    Released only on the owner’s approval

    Teasers can be public. Financial models and permits are requested, and the owner decides who sees them.

  • EVERY DOWNLOAD

    Logged, so owners can see who has what

    Who downloaded a document and when is recorded and shown to the listing owner.
